# Hookline — launch film plan

**What it is:** a professional weather radar for Android that you buy once: every NEXRAD and open European radar, every tilt and product, warnings and Storm Mode, no subscription.
**For:** storm chasers, spotters and weather nerds in the US and Europe who are tired of paying twice a year for public data.
**Sets it apart:** pro radar depth (raw tilts, velocity, dual-pol, storm tracking) for one price; US and Europe in one app.
**Hook:** the radar beam paints a real-looking supercell, and the film teaches the name: "This is a hook echo."
**Visual identity:** app night palette (#0A0E12 / #141B22, bone #ECE8DF, signal red #E4472F), Inter Tight + IBM Plex Mono, the couplet mark (split disc in a bearing scale), the app's real NWS reflectivity / CVD velocity / CC palettes on polar-binned radar.
**Tone:** cinematic-polished: precise, instrument-like, fast cuts on the beat, no gimmicks.
**Share line:** "Hookline: professional weather radar you buy once."
